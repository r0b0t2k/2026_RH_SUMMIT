The Three Scorers We'll Use
Domain F1
Do answers match the ground-truth facts? 
Token F1 vs policy.md.
Score: 0–1
▪ 1.0 = answer perfectly matches ground 
truth word-for-word
▪ 0.0 = no overlap with ground truth
▪ Threshold: ≥ 0.80
Catches: wrong dates and missing info
Start here. Add domain-speciﬁc scorers as you discover.
What does EDD actually measure for our demo?
Consistency (Jaccard)
Do consecutive answers agree? 
Same question, 5 runs.
Score: 0–1
▪ 1.0 = identical answers every run
▪ 0.0 = no words in common between any 
pair
▪ Threshold: ≥ 0.50
Catches: non-determinism
SCORERS
7
Confabulation
Does any answer invent facts not in the 
source?
Score: 0–1
▪ 1.0 = zero answers contained hallucinated 
facts (all clean)
▪ 0.0 = every answer contained a 
hallucinated date or number
▪ Computed as: fraction of answers with 
NO detected confabulation
▪ Threshold: ≥ 0.90
Catches: hallucinated dates and numbers

Session ID

Title
Evaluation-driven development (EDD): AI applications that don't lie
Abstract
Your tests are green. Your retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipeline passed every query in your validation set. Two weeks 
post-launch: hallucinations, stale answers, retrieval gaps, the demo never surfaced. 
The problem is the assumption underlying your tests—that determinism means correctness. Language models don't work that 
way. Neither do RAG pipelines.
Evaluation-driven development (EDD) is the ﬁx. In this live-coding session, we'll break test-driven development (TDD) against 
a real large language model (LLM), then rebuild a RAG pipeline with a structured evaluation loop using 2 open source tools: 
SDG Hub, a synthetic data generation (SDG) toolkit, which produces domain-speciﬁc queries and ground-truth answers at 
scale
EvalHub, the open source uniﬁed AI evaluation platform, which benchmarks faithfulness and retrieval quality, surfaces 
hallucinations and retrieval gaps, and runs the same evals at production scale on Kubernetes.
Pull out your laptop. We're writing evals from scratch. You'll leave with an eval harness, an SDG Hub ﬂow, and a ﬁrst look at 
EvalHub.
